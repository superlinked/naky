# Demo recording attribution

## GUI-World — iOS

The demo uses `IOS/330.mp4` from the [GUI-World dataset by
ONE-Lab](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ONE-Lab/GUI-World), pinned at revision
`7901fa3f0b9e36afa87d24ff9ecd48037c7fd6a0` from the TRAIN/development split.
GUI-World is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Näky canonicalized the recording to AV1 before producing the ScreenEvents and
state text displayed on the page. The original MP4 is included byte-for-byte
for broad browser playback. The overlay and state text are derived
representations of that recording.

Third-party names, marks, and interface content belong to their respective
owners. This demo contains no benchmark question, answer, annotation, model
response, or user account data.

## Android in the Wild — Android

The Android example uses ten released screenshots from episode
`6456252662470590836` in the General Standard.train split of [Android in the
Wild](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/5b09c22d73a9d35eb6c5d2a99b95677a45053466/android_in_the_wild),
pinned to Google Research revision
`5b09c22d73a9d35eb6c5d2a99b95677a45053466`. Google Research releases datasets
in that repository under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The benchmark source is a screenshot sequence rather than a continuous video.
For this page, its ten frames are presented at one frame per second without
resizing or cropping, then canonicalized to AV1 for Näky. The page contains no
task, action, answer, outcome, annotation, or model response from the benchmark.
